Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free: 1 pet, up to 2 care tasks, unlimited medications, weight tracking, health records.

Pro (One-Time Purchase): Unlimited pets, unlimited care tasks, breathing log, vet visit tracking, pet sitter reports, medication adherence %, CSV export.

No subscriptions! Pay once and own forever.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

When you try to add a second pet, a third care task, create a pet sitter report, or add a vet visit, the app will show you the upgrade screen. You can also upgrade from Settings > Upgrade to Pro.

How do I add a pet?

Tap the "+" button on the main Pets screen. Fill in your pet's information including name, species, breed, date of birth, and photo. Free users can add 1 pet; Pro users can add unlimited pets.

How do I schedule medications?

Open your pet's detail page, tap "Add Medication" in the Medications section. Enter the medication name, dosage, and instructions. Then add schedule times with your preferred frequency (daily, weekly, custom). You must specify how many times or until when the schedule should repeat.

How do I mark a medication as taken?

Go to the Reminders tab and swipe right on any reminder to mark it as taken in one gesture, or tap the "Mark Taken" button. The app will warn you if you're marking too early, too late, or if it's already been taken today.

What is the app icon badge?

The badge on Mascotty's icon shows how many reminders are currently overdue. It updates automatically whenever you open the app or mark a reminder as taken. It clears to zero when nothing is overdue.

Can I track grooming and other care tasks?

Yes! When adding a medication, toggle the type to "Care Task". You can schedule grooming, nail trimming, ear cleaning, baths, flea treatments, and more. Free users can track up to 2 care tasks; Pro users have unlimited care tasks.

How does the breathing log work?

The breathing log is a Pro feature. On your pet's detail page, open the Breathing Log section to add resting breathing rate entries. The chart shows your entries over time and displays a shaded reference band for your pet's species' normal resting range (where applicable). You can also export the log as a CSV file.

How do I schedule vet visits?

Vet visit tracking is a Pro feature. On your pet's detail page, scroll to the Upcoming Vet Visits section. Tap "Add Vet Visit", select the date and time, and add an optional reason. You'll receive a reminder 24 hours before the appointment.

How do I generate a pet sitter report?

Pet sitter reports are a Pro feature. Open your pet's detail page and tap "Pet Sitter Report". Select which pets to include, choose your date range (maximum 90 days), and tap "Generate Report". You can then preview and share the report via message or email.

Why do I need to set an end condition for schedules?

To prevent notification overflow, all recurring schedules must have either a repeat count (e.g., 30 times) or an end date. This protects your device from being flooded with unlimited reminders.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All data is stored locally on your device only. Mascotty never uploads your data to servers or shares it with third parties. Your pets' health information stays completely private.
